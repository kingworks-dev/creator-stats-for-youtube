# Privacy Policy: Creator Stats for YouTube

Effective November 20, 2026. Publisher: KING HOLDINGS VENTURES LLC (King AI). Contact: hello@usekingai.com

Creator Stats for YouTube does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. The extension has no servers, no analytics, no accounts and makes no network requests.

What it reads: on youtube.com and studio.youtube.com a content script reads text already rendered on the page you are viewing (title, view count, publish date, like button label, tags, description, and the video list) to compute the numbers shown in its panel. That data is not stored or sent anywhere. It does not use the YouTube API.

What it stores: your Pro key if you enter one, in the browser's local extension storage (`storage.local`). Removing the extension deletes it. A CSV is created only when you click Export and is saved by your browser to your device.

Pro key: the key is checked on your device and never sent anywhere.

Not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Questions or deletion requests: hello@usekingai.com
